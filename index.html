                01 — About
                
Where hardware meets software.

            

            

                

                    

                        I'm an Electrical & Electronics Engineer — graduated with Honors (3.34/4.00) from
                        Karadeniz Technical University's 100% English programme — currently working as a
                        Junior R&D Specialist at Aygün Surgical Instruments, where I develop embedded
                        systems for surgical motors and medical devices. In parallel, I'm pursuing my M.Sc. at Ondokuz Mayıs University.
                    

                    

                        My focus is embedded firmware, real-time control and embedded Linux. I've integrated
                        LiDAR, stereo vision and 5G on an autonomous ground vehicle during my Erasmus+ internship in Portugal,
                        built STM32 motor control over CAN bus and Modbus as a TÜBİTAK researcher, and developed robots
                        ranging from pipe-inspection vehicles to a ROS 2 quadruped.
                    

                    

                        My goal is to create impactful technology by bridging the gap between hardware and software —
                        combining technical rigor with a bias for building things that actually work.
